# Madison Armstrong - Portfolio

A minimal, editorial portfolio for Madison Armstrong, built with Next.js (App Router), TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS v4.

Design language follows the aesthetic of her Parsons capstone deck (Mischief Maison Studio): black, deep red, off-white/grey, vintage editorial mood with high-low contrast. Typography pairs Fraunces (display serif) with Inter (body), on a major-third type scale, with scroll-reveal motion that respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Content provenance

All copy and imagery come from materials Madison emailed:

- `Mischief Maison Studio` capstone (Parsons Fashion Business Essentials, Aug 2026): brand overview, problem/solution, reimagine-or-redirect model, target customer, marketing strategy, funding request, three-year revenue forecast, and the product line inspiration mood board.
- Customer Journey Map (Reflora, Jul 2026)
- Digital Product Passport proposal (The Impact of Retail Technology, Jul 2026)
- Chanel brand timeline (Nov 2025)
- Lookbook (nine curated outfits)
- Resume (experience and education)
